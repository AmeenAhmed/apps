Vyber support

Vyber makes personal applets from your own words: trackers, logs, checklists, planners, timers and calculators for your own life. Describe one, or start from a ready-made applet, and change it until it fits. Your applets and everything you save in them stay on your phone.

A question, an applet that won't behave, or a purchase that went wrong? Email cleverapestudio@gmail.com. You'll hear back from the person who makes the app, usually within two days.

Credits and purchases

What does a credit buy?

One new applet, or one change to an applet, whatever its size. A comment you pin to part of an applet is a change too. Opening, using and undoing applets is free, and so is everything in the ready-made applets until you ask for a change.

Do I pay when something goes wrong?

No. If the builder can't finish, if you stop it, or if Vyber declines the request, the credit comes back straight away.

How do I get more credits?

Vyber gives 10 credits the first time you open it. After that, Vyber Plus adds 100 credits every month, for $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year. Plus credits are used before any you buy, and ones left at the end of a month don't carry over. Or buy packs of 15, 50 and 120 credits, which never expire. Both are in Settings, and on the build screen when you run out.

How do I cancel Vyber Plus?

In Vyber, open Settings and tap Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before it renews; Plus keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for.

I have a new phone, or reinstalled Vyber. Where are my credits?

Your balance belongs to the Apple Account you downloaded Vyber with, not to the phone. Open Vyber on the new phone with the same Apple Account and the balance comes back by itself, and so does Vyber Plus. If Plus doesn't show, tap Restore purchases under the plans in Settings. The free credits come once per Apple Account.

Can I get a refund?

Apple takes the payment, so Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Making and changing applets

What can Vyber make?

Tools for one person's own life: trackers and logs, habits and routines, checklists and to-do lists, planners and schedules, timers, calculators and converters, flashcards, journals and sketch pads. And simple games to play on your phone, alone or passing it around: board, card, puzzle, word and arcade games. Each one works offline and keeps its records on your phone.

What won't it make?

Chatbots, anything that needs the internet, gambling or casino games (even for pretend money), moving real money, measuring your body with the phone or diagnosing, fakes of real things, watching other people, anything unsuitable for 13 and up, and apps meant for other people. Vyber declines these without charging a credit and suggests something it could make instead. It won't copy someone else's game either: ask for one and you get an original game with the same kind of play.

How do I change an applet?

Open it and tap the sparkles at the top right, then say what should be different. To point at the part you mean, tap the speech bubble, tap that part of the applet and write your comment; a numbered bubble stays on it and shows the builder's answers. Each change shows what was done and has an Undo. For a ready-made applet, Restore the original in its menu takes it back to how it came.

An applet doesn't work the way I asked.

Comment on the part that's wrong, or describe the problem with the sparkles: the builder gets the applet as it is and works from there. If it keeps going wrong, use Report a problem in the applet's menu (the three dots) and choose It doesn’t work.

Reminders don't arrive.

Check Settings → Notifications → Vyber on your iPhone, then Reminders in Vyber's Settings, where each applet's reminders can be turned off. iOS keeps at most 64 upcoming notifications per app, so with many reminders across applets, the latest ones wait until earlier ones have gone off.

Your data and safety

Where are my applets saved?

On your phone, in Vyber's private storage. Applets can't reach the internet, so what you save in them never leaves the phone. Deleting an applet deletes what it saved, and deleting Vyber deletes everything. An iPhone backup may include them, under Apple's own terms.

What does the AI see?

Only what you write when you ask for an applet or a change, and the applet's own code. Never your entries, photos or settings, and never who you are. The details are in the privacy policy.

How do I report an applet?

Open its menu (the three dots) and tap Report a problem. Choose what's wrong and add a note if you like. Every report is read by a person within two days, and builds can be closed to anyone who misuses them.
