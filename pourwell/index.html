Pourwell support

Pourwell is a candle making calculator for home and small-batch candle makers: wax and fragrance oil for every pour, what each candle costs you, and a log of batches while they cure.

A question, a bug, or a number that looks wrong? Email cleverapestudio@gmail.com. You'll hear back from the person who makes the app, usually within a few days.

Pro and purchases

I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new phone?

Open Pourwell, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pourwell. On Android: open the Play Store, tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. You keep Pro until the end of the year you paid for. The lifetime purchase never renews, so there's nothing to cancel.

Can I get a refund?

Apple and Google take the payment, so they handle refunds. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, Google Play usually refunds within 48 hours of purchase from your order history; after that, email me and I'll sort it out.

How the numbers work

Why is the wax weight less than my jar's size?

Wax is lighter than water. Soy wax weighs about 0.86 g per ml, so an 8 fl oz jar holds about 7.2 oz of soy by weight. Each wax has its own fill factor, and you can change it if your supplier gives a different figure or you fill below the rim.

How is fragrance load worked out?

As a percentage of the wax weight, the way fragrance suppliers quote it: at 8%, every 100 g of wax takes 8 g of oil. Pourwell then splits each candle's fill so that wax and oil together fill the jar exactly, never more.

Where are my batches saved?

Only on your phone. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes your batches, so keep the app installed if you want to keep your log.
