Pourwell privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026

Pourwell doesn't collect your recipes, your batches or anything that identifies you. There's no account, no ads and no analytics. What you type stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your batches, scents, notes, prices and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are never sent to me or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your phone with Apple or Google, the backup may include them, under Apple's or Google's own terms.

Purchases

If you buy Pourwell Pro, Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) takes the payment. I never see your name, email or payment details.

To check what you've bought, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. When the app starts, and when you buy or restore, it sends RevenueCat:

  • a random ID the app makes up for itself, not linked to your name or email;
  • your purchase receipts and purchase history from the store;
  • technical details: app version, operating system version, device model, store country and language, and your IP address, as with any internet connection.

RevenueCat uses this only to confirm which purchases you've made, and I use its reports to see how many people buy Pro. The legal basis is delivering what you paid for.

Ratings

Now and then the app asks Apple or Google to show their standard rating prompt. They run that prompt; Pourwell never sees whether or how you rated it.

What Pourwell never touches

Your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or advertising ID. Pourwell doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Pourwell isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything about them.

Your choices

Everything you enter is on your phone, where you can change or delete it at any time. To have RevenueCat's record of your purchases deleted, email me and I'll arrange it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
