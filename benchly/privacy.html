Benchly privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Benchly doesn't collect your players' names, your games or anything that identifies you. There's no account, no ads and no analytics. What you type stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your teams, the players' names, shirt numbers and positions, your game plans, minutes played and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are never sent to me or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your phone with Apple, the backup may include them, under Apple's own terms.

Purchases

If you buy Benchly Pro, Apple takes the payment through the App Store. I never see your name, email or payment details.

To check what you've bought, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. When the app starts, and when you buy or restore, it sends RevenueCat:

  • a random ID the app makes up for itself, not linked to your name or email;
  • your purchase receipts and purchase history from the store;
  • technical details: app version, operating system version, device model, store country and language, and your IP address, as with any internet connection.

RevenueCat uses this only to confirm which purchases you've made, and I use its reports to see how many people buy Pro. Nothing about your team or players is ever sent. The legal basis is delivering what you paid for.

Ratings

Now and then the app asks Apple to show its standard rating prompt. Apple runs that prompt; Benchly never sees whether or how you rated it.

What Benchly never touches

Your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or advertising ID. Benchly doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Benchly is made for the adults who coach youth teams, not for children, and it doesn't knowingly collect anything about children. The players' names a coach types in stay on the coach's phone and are never sent anywhere.

Your choices

Everything you enter is on your phone, where you can change or delete it at any time: remove a player, delete a game or delete a whole team. To have RevenueCat's record of your purchases deleted, email me and I'll arrange it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
