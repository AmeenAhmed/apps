Benchly support

Benchly is a soccer lineup builder for youth coaches: it plans every substitution so playing time comes out fair, keeps the clock on the sideline, and adds up each player's minutes across the season.

A question, a bug, or a plan that looks wrong? Email cleverapestudio@gmail.com. You'll hear back from the person who makes the app, usually within a few days.

Pro and purchases

I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new phone?

Open Benchly, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Benchly. You keep Pro until the end of the year you paid for. The lifetime purchase never renews, so there's nothing to cancel.

Can I get a refund?

Apple takes the payment, so Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How the plan works

How does Benchly decide who plays when?

It splits each half or quarter into the number of lineups you choose and fills them so that everyone who's there gets as close to the same minutes as possible. Where it can, it puts players in the positions you marked them as preferring. Tap any place on the pitch to change who plays there, and the lineups after it even out again.

Why don't the minutes come out exactly equal?

With 10 players for 7 places, not everyone can play the same number of minutes, so the plan gets as close as the number of lineups allows. More lineups per half gives a closer split; fewer means fewer interruptions to the game.

How is the keeper chosen?

At the start of each half or quarter, from the players you marked as able to play keeper, the one with the least time in goal so far. They stay in goal for that period.

A player arrived late or had to leave. What do I do?

Before kickoff, untick them under Who's here. When they turn up, tap Arrived: it's under the plan, or under the bench once the game has started. If someone has to leave during the game, tap them on the pitch and choose has left or is hurt. Either way, the rest of the game is re-planned around who's there.

Does the clock keep running if my phone locks?

Yes. The clock is worked out from the time you started, so it stays right if the phone locks or you switch apps. The screen stays awake while the clock is running.

Where is my team saved?

Only on your phone. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes your team and its games, so keep the app installed if you want to keep your season.
